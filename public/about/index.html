About Repeater

Supply, on trade terms.

Repeater is an Australian wholesale supplier to repair businesses. The range is screens, batteries, boards and consumables — the lines a bench gets through — sold on trade terms rather than at a retail counter.

Wholesale is a different job from retail. A repair business isn't buying a part for one device; it's buying the line that keeps a bench busy, and it needs to know the price before the customer says yes. That's a supply problem, not a shopfront one, and it's the thing this business is built around.

The account model exists for the same reason. Terms mean a busy week doesn't turn into a cashflow problem, quantity breaks mean ordering more actually costs less, and one statement means the month reconciles in one place instead of five.

Trade supply

What a trade account covers.

This is the parts side of Repeater, the supply line behind the phones, the AI and the OS. The range is drawn from live trade stock, so what's available moves with what's actually on the shelf. What you pay is set per account: your terms and volume decide the price, which is why nothing here carries one.

What a trade account covers

Three kinds of supply.

The account is built around what a repair bench gets through, not a catalogue padded with variants nobody orders twice. Availability tracks live trade stock.

01

Screens & display assemblies

Display assemblies and the parts that go with them, held against live trade stock. What's listed is what's on the shelf rather than a static range that quietly went out of stock.

02

Batteries & power

Replacement batteries, power assemblies and charging parts, the lines that decide whether a repair is worth taking on a given day.

03

Boards, tools & consumables

Boards and the bench consumables a repair business gets through every week, ordered alongside the parts so it arrives as one delivery instead of four.

Trade pricing is released to approved accounts.

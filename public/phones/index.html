PA-1 · Fleet Phones

Fleet Phones — device only.

$622 all-in per device on signing — device, setup and fleet administration for the whole term.

$572 device + $50 establishment + $27.50 fleet administration fee (one-off, capped at $110 per plan for the whole term). Day one is $649.50 per device at 2–4 devices, less at 5+ as the cap spreads.

No weekly fee. 24 months. No repair services included: manufacturer-warranty faults are administered free, and any other repair is quoted and approved before work starts. PA-2 · Managed Fleet

Managed Fleet — from $5.60 a week per device.

$622 upfront per device, then from $5.60 a week per device — that is the care: repairs, call-outs and the loaner swap, plus fleet administration spread across your weekly payments ($27.50 per device for the whole term, capped at $110 per plan). 24 months.

Repairs included: 2 service events per quarter, plus 1 extra per quarter for every 4 devices on the plan, $0 each. Past the cap? $60 flat per extra repair. First 2 call-outs free every 12 months, then $19 within 20 km. Can't fix it same day? A loaner goes out the same visit. MDM-managed and PPSR-registered. The phone stays Repeater's property — you never own it. Pay in full and it is 10% off your tier's total.

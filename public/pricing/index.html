Pricing

What everything costs.

Every price Repeater publishes, on one page. Two phone plans, one coming soon, and the shop services — each with its own terms. The hard parts are here on page one: the minimum terms, the upfront and weekly amounts, the caps, the call-out fee, and the fact that the phones stay ours.

Minimum two devices on any phone plan, fixed for the whole 24-month term. The shop services run on a 13-week minimum, then cancel any one of them on its own. All amounts include GST.

PA-1 · Fleet Phones

Fleet Phones — device only.

$622 all-in per device on signing — device, setup and fleet administration for the whole term. No weekly fee. 24 months. Hand the phones back at term end and start fresh on the current model, or renew. Warranty faults are handled for you free. Any other repair is quoted before work starts. Phone stolen or lost? A $300 fleet loss fee ends that device's lease, and there is no payout of the remaining term. MDM-managed and PPSR-registered. Care is not available on this plan — choose Managed Fleet if you want repairs included. Your plan is fixed for the term.

PA-2 · Managed Fleet

Managed Fleet — from $5.60 a week per device.

$622 upfront per device. From $5.60 a week per device — that is the care: repairs, call-outs and the loaner swap — plus fleet administration spread across your weekly payments ($27.50 per device for the whole term, capped at $110 per plan). 24 months. The phone stays Repeater's property — you never own it, we manage everything. Repairs included: 2 service events per quarter, plus 1 extra per quarter for every 4 devices on the plan, $0 each. Past the cap? $60 flat per extra repair. First 2 call-outs free every 12 months, then $19 within 20 km. Can't fix it same day? You get a loaner phone the same visit. MDM-managed and PPSR-registered. At term end hand it back and start fresh on the current model.

Why a fleet plan

Employees break phones. That is the whole problem.

On a Managed Fleet plan every device carries included repairs, so a cracked screen is not a conversation about who pays. The fleet stays Repeater's property under MDM: we keep it enrolled, we handle the fix, and when a phone can't be repaired the same day it is swapped from the loaner pool so the crew keeps working.

Missing one $500 job a week costs you $26,000 a year. A fleet plan is the smaller number.

01

2 service events per quarter, plus 1 extra per quarter for every 4 devices. $0 each, up to the cap.

02

$60 flat past the cap

Extra repairs are $60 flat, up to the 4-paid-events limit per device in a rolling 12 months.

03

Loaner the same visit

If it can't be fixed same day, a refurb loaner goes out from the pool and the job carries on.

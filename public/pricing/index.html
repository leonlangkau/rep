How trade pricing resolves.

Volume breaks

Breaks apply automatically.

A quantity break is a price step tied to how much of a line you order. It isn't negotiated per order: it sits in the fixed resolution order, applies as the quantity grows, and the best applicable price wins on its own.

Standard terms run at 7, 14, 30 or 60 days depending on the account. New accounts normally start shorter and extend as a payment history builds.

The credit limit is set per account and enforced as a hard stop rather than a warning. It caps how far an account can run at any time, which is what keeps a bad week from becoming a bad quarter.
